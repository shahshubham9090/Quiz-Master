FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Quiz Master free?

Yes. Quiz Master is free to download and use. A small banner ad helps keep it that way.

Who is Quiz Master for?

Students who want to practise and revise, teachers who want to set quizzes and support their class, and schools or coaching centres that want to run everything in one place.

How do I join my teacher's class?

Ask your teacher for the group code and enter it in the Groups section of the app. Your teacher can also invite you directly, and you just tap Accept.

Can teachers create their own quizzes?

Yes. Teachers can create quizzes, share study materials and answer doubts. Students are notified when new work is posted.

Is it available on iPhone?

Quiz Master is available on Android through the Google Play Store today.

Is my data safe? Can I delete my account?

Your data is stored securely on Google Firebase and is never sold. You can delete your account at any time from Profile → Delete Account. Read our Privacy Policy or how to delete your account.
