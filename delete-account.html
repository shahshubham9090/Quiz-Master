How to permanently remove your Quiz Master account and data.

Delete from the app

  1. Open Quiz Master and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Delete Account.
  3. Confirm with your password, or choose your Google account if you signed in with Google.

Your account and data are deleted immediately.

Can't open the app? Request by email

  1. Send an email to shah123shubham@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Quiz Master account".
  2. Send it from the email address you use to sign in, so we can confirm the account is yours.
  3. We will confirm by email and delete your account within 30 days.

Email a deletion request

What we delete

  • Your account and sign-in details (email and Google sign-in)
  • Your profile: name, avatar and role
  • Your quiz attempts, scores, points and game high scores
  • Doubts you have posted
  • Your membership in study groups

What we may keep

Quizzes and study materials created by teachers or admins may stay in the app for other students after the author's account is deleted. Tell us in your email if you want these removed too.

Backups are overwritten within 30 days of deletion. Advertising data collected by Google AdMob is handled by Google. You can reset your advertising ID in your Android settings under Google → Ads.

For more details, see our Privacy Policy.
