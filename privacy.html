Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Quiz Master mobile app ("the app", "we", "us") collects, uses and protects your information. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

InformationWhy we use it
Name and email addressTo create and manage your account and show your name to your teachers and group members.
Google account details (if you sign in with Google)Your name, email and Google account ID, used only to sign you in.
Account role (student, teacher or admin)To show you the right features.
Quiz attempts, scores and pointsTo show your results, progress charts and leaderboards.
Game high scoresTo show game leaderboards.
Doubts you post and repliesSo teachers can answer your questions.
Study group membershipTo connect you with your teacher and classmates.
Device notification tokenTo send you notifications about new quizzes and materials.
Advertising ID and device informationCollected by Google AdMob to show ads (see section 3).

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos or phone number.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide the app's features: quizzes, results, study materials, doubts, groups and leaderboards.
  • To let teachers and admins see the progress of students in their classes.
  • To send notifications about new content. You can turn these off in your phone's settings.
  • To show ads, which keep the app free.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Third-party services

The app uses these services, which process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Messaging), which stores your account and app data and delivers notifications. Firebase privacy
  • Google Sign-In, used if you choose to sign in with Google. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob, which shows ads and may use your device's advertising ID. How Google uses data in ads
  • DiceBear, which generates cartoon profile avatars. An anonymous seed value is sent to create the image. DiceBear privacy

You can reset or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings under Google → Ads.

4. Who can see your information

  • Teachers can see the name, email, scores and results of students in their groups.
  • Admins can see all users, quiz attempts and doubts in order to run the platform.
  • Other students can see your display name, avatar and points on leaderboards and in groups you share.

5. Data storage and security

Your data is stored on Google Firebase servers and sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access is limited by security rules based on your role. No system is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

6. Data retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can ask us to delete your account and all related data at any time. See Delete your account for how. We complete deletion requests within 30 days.

7. Children

The app is made for school students. If you are under the age required to consent in your country, please use the app with permission from a parent, guardian or your school. We do not knowingly collect more information from children than the app needs to work. If you believe a child has given us information without permission, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal information. You can edit your name and avatar in the app's profile screen. For anything else, email us.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the effective date above, and for major changes we will notify you in the app.
